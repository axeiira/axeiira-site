Build a single-page personal portfolio website with a minimalist monospace / terminal aesthetic.
Typography: Use a monospaced font throughout (e.g. JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, or Geist Mono). All body text lowercase except the name. Keep font sizes small and restrained; rely on weight (regular vs. bold) and color for hierarchy rather than size.
Layout: A single narrow centered column (~600px max width) on a full-width dark canvas. Generous vertical whitespace between sections. Left-aligned text. No cards, no borders except a single thin horizontal divider under the intro.
Color: Near-black background (#0d0d0d–#111). Off-white primary text, muted gray secondary text. One subtle accent color (soft lavender/purple) used only for small section markers. Provide a light/dark toggle (sun/moon icon, top right).
Sections (top to bottom):

Name (bold, larger) + location and availability status with tiny inline icons
A short 2–3 line bio with one or two bold phrases for emphasis
^ currently — what you're working on now
^ work — job entries with role underneath and right-aligned muted date ranges
^ projects — project name + one-line description, right-aligned year
Footer: small email and GitHub icons, centered

Each section header is prefixed with a small lavender ^ glyph. Dates and years are right-aligned and dimmed. Keep everything calm, sparse, and typography-driven — no shadows, gradients, or decorative graphics. Fully responsive, accessible contrast, smooth theme transition.
